Startup · Founder

THE SMART

An Arabic-first exam-prep platform for the Algerian baccalauréat. Students practise on real past papers, take a daily quiz, follow chapter-ordered lessons and ask an AI tutor that answers the way an exam paper is written.

  • AI tutor and exam solver on Google Gemini, with solutions pre-computed and cached for every paper
  • Grading runs inside Postgres — the answer key never reaches the browser
  • 210 attack tests as real signed-in users: privilege escalation, IDOR, score forgery, SSRF
  • Flashcards, mistake log, spaced review, weekly reports, leaderboard and streaks
