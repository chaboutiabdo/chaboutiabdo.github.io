Meet THE SMART AI

The AI inside THE SMART is built for one job: helping Algerian students pass the BAC. It speaks their language, follows their curriculum, and explains every answer the way an exam paper expects.

Arabic explanations, Latin notation, one step at a time — the way a BAC paper is written.

Four ways it helps

AI tutor

Pick a subject and a chapter, then ask anything. It answers in Arabic, step by step, and stays inside the chapter you chose.

Explain my mistake

Every question you get wrong can be explained in one tap: what went wrong and the right method. The mistake then comes back for review a few days later.

AI flashcards

Generate a set of flashcards for any chapter, straight into your personal deck — no duplicates of cards you already have.

AI exam solver

Open any past BAC paper and get a full solution, step by step. When an official solution exists, the AI explains it — and it always tells you which one you’re reading.

The principles behind it

  • Grounded, not guessing. Official solutions come first. The AI teaches the method examiners expect, not a shortcut that loses marks.
  • Steps you can check. Every answer is broken into steps, so students learn the method instead of copying a final number.
  • Built around the BAC programme. Subjects and chapters come from the official curriculum, so every conversation stays focused on what’s actually in the exam.
  • Private by design. Your questions and your mistakes are yours. No student can see another student’s data.
  • Fast for everyone. Solutions for past papers are prepared in advance, so they open instantly, and fair-use limits keep the service quick at exam time.
  • Tested like an attacker would. The AI features are part of THE SMART’s security suite — see I attacked my own app 210 times.
